About Us

Georgia Surplus Fund Recovery, Statewide

When a Georgia property is sold at a tax sale for more than the taxes and costs owed, the extra money — called excess funds — is held by the county tax commissioner or sheriff. Under O.C.G.A. § 48-4-5, it belongs to the owner and lienholders of record at the time of the sale.

At Rightful Returns Recovery, we locate tax sale excess funds across all 159 Georgia counties and help former owners and their heirs claim them.

There is no cost to you upfront. We work on contingency — we only earn a fee if we successfully recover money for you.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What Georgia law covers surplus funds?

O.C.G.A. § 48-4-5 governs excess funds from tax sales in Georgia. The funds are paid to the owner and lienholders of record at the time of the sale, in order of priority.

How long do I have to claim surplus funds in Georgia?

The county holds tax sale excess funds for five years from the sale date. After that, unclaimed funds go to the Georgia Department of Revenue, and they can only be released by a court order from an interpleader action filed in the county where the sale took place. Acting within the five years is much simpler.

Which Georgia counties do you cover?

All 159 Georgia counties, including Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb, Gwinnett, Clayton, Cherokee, Forsyth, Hall, Chatham (Savannah), and more.

What if no funds are found?

There is no obligation and no fee. Our search is completely free and risk-free for you.
